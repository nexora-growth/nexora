// Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct operational clarity for developers, EPCs, and execution contractors.

Do you charge any upfront registration or listing fees?
No. NEXORA operates on a strictly success-based, Paid-as-Paid framework. Our facilitation fee applies only upon awarded contracts and client milestone disbursements.
How are subcontractor credentials and capacity verified?
Every execution partner in our network is audited for valid electrical contractor licenses, GST/MSME documentation, owned machinery base (piling rigs/testing tools), and completed MW track records.
How quickly can execution gangs be mobilized on-site?
Depending on location and scope, regional teams across Rajasthan, Gujarat, Haryana, UP, and Odisha can be mobilized within 5 to 10 days post work-order finalization.
How are commercial rates and client relationships protected?
All technical and pricing disclosures are preceded by standard Mutual Non-Circumvention Non-Disclosure Agreements (NCNDA) executed directly between leadership desks.
