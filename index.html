About NEXORA

One sourcing desk for project and procurement needs.

NEXORA is an institutional project sourcing and subcontractor desk operated under ALEX VK ELECTRICALS. We bridge principal EPC contractors and developers with pre-vetted regional execution subcontractors and certified industrial equipment manufacturers.

We work to understand the project scope, location, technical requirements and timeline, then help identify potential partners for review and direct commercial discussion.

  • Requirement-led contractor and supplier search
  • Connections across civil, electrical, solar and industrial sectors
  • Manufacturer representation & BOQ sourcing support
  • Standard Mutual NCNDA & transparent Paid-as-Paid frameworks
